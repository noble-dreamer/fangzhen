\begin{tabular}{>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{46mm} >{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{350mm}}
\toprule
Block & Stack rule in code \\
\midrule
ResBlock & GroupNorm + SiLU + Conv, then FiLM-conditioned GroupNorm, SiLU + Dropout + Conv, plus identity or 1x1 skip. Every ResBlock receives $c=e_x+e_t$. \\
PicAdapter add & After every ResBlock, the U-Net adds the PicEncoder zero-conv feature matching the current level. It is bilinearly resized only if the spatial size differs. \\
AttentionBlock & Self-attention over flattened $H \times W$ locations. In the base48 config it is used at resolution 32 and in the middle block. \\
CrossAttentionBlock & Queries come from normalized U-Net spatial tokens. Keys and values come from TX-RX x tokens. It is used at resolution 32 and in the middle block. \\
Downsample & 3x3 stride-2 convolution. Channels stay the same; resolution halves. \\
Upsample & nearest-neighbor upsample by 2, then 3x3 convolution. Channels stay the same; resolution doubles. \\
Skip concatenation & Decoder ResBlocks concatenate the current feature with the latest saved encoder skip tensor along channel dimension before the ResBlock. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
